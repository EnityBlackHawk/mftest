### Workload
- **Used 100% of the time:**
```sql
SELECT e.eventname, SUM(s.qtysold) AS total_tickets, 
       SUM(s.pricepaid) AS total_revenue,
       SUM(s.pricepaid) - SUM(s.commission) AS net_revenue
FROM sales s
JOIN event e ON s.eventid = e.eventid
GROUP BY e.eventname
ORDER BY net_revenue DESC;
```

### LLM Explanation
We embed sales documents within each event to optimize the heavy aggregation of sales by event name, and also embed venue and date data for the frequent co-access with events. Other entities like users, categories, listings, and dates remain as separate collections with references to balance reuse and updates.
